# Clear Waters promotional cover

[Cover image](clear-waters-cover.png) is the first image for the mod listing. It
was created with the built-in imagegen tool on October 4, 2026, using the
[mirror screenshot with the filter on](screenshots/clear-waters-mirror-on.jpg)
as the edit target. It is illustrated promotional artwork, not a gameplay
capture. The separate screenshot comparisons document the mod's actual effects.

The cover uses the mod name, the existing “A calmer, clearer view.” line, and a
pixel-style version of the water-drop mark. The name remains the dominant
element when viewed as a small listing thumbnail.

For context, the [Super Dave](https://www.nexusmods.com/davethediver/mods/4) and
[SuperDave 2.0](https://www.nexusmods.com/davethediver/mods/17) Nexus covers
were reviewed as examples of artwork used to identify a mod. Their assets were
not used in this cover.

## Exact generation prompt

```text
Use case: ads-marketing.
Asset type: the main promotional cover and listing thumbnail for the Clear Waters mod for Dave the Diver.
Primary request: Transform the supplied gameplay screenshot into polished promotional title artwork. This is an artistic cover, so you may recompose and repaint the scene; preserve the recognizable game setting and Dave's character identity rather than exact screenshot pixels.
Input image 1: edit target and reference for the underwater ice cavern, glowing circular mirror, chunky rock shapes, Dave, and game colors.
Canvas: landscape 16:9, ideally 1920 by 1080.
Art direction: a striking, clean game-mod cover with the game's charming pixel-influenced illustration style. Deep ocean navy, blue and turquoise with pale mint accents, matching the Clear Waters identity. Crisp rock and ice silhouettes, restrained cyan light from the mirror, Dave in his dark diving suit and yellow goggles visible at the right. Simplify the environment so the title dominates and remains readable at a 320-pixel-wide thumbnail.
Composition: prominent typography in the center-left, the mirror and Dave supporting it at the right. Keep important content comfortably inside the edges. Use a compact two-line title, with strong, bold, slightly blocky lettering in warm white and pale mint, clean dark edging for contrast. A small teal water-drop emblem with a single white wave can accompany the title.
Text, exactly and only:
"DAVE THE DIVER" as a small game identifier above the title.
"CLEAR" on the first large title line and "WATERS" on the second large title line.
"A calmer, clearer view." as one short line below.
Constraints: remove every original HUD element, oxygen meter, number, controller prompt, bar, menu, and screenshot overlay. No before/after panels, labels, technical feature lists, health promises, website addresses, or extra characters. No photographic realism, photoreal water, excessive bloom, smeared edges, motion blur, chromatic aberration, or rainbow glitches. This should feel welcoming, calm, and sharply legible, with professional promotional composition.
```
